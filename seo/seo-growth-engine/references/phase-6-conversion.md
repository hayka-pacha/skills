# Phase 6 — Conversion Optimization

SEO drives clicks; conversion drives revenue. Optimize both — they reinforce each other (user signals feed back into ranking).

## 6.1 CTA Placement

Three CTA positions per page:
1. **Above the fold** — hero section, immediately visible without scrolling
2. **Mid-content** — after key information, at a natural break
3. **Bottom** — final push after the user has read the case

CTA copy that converts:
- Action verb + benefit: "Start free trial", "See plans", "Add to cart", "Get the report"
- Urgency when real: "Sale ends Sunday", "Low stock", limited-time. Don't fake urgency — users notice.
- Personalize: use product name, plan tier, user segment when relevant ("Start your Pro trial" beats "Start trial")

Avoid: generic "Learn more", "Click here", "Submit". They underperform consistently in CXL/Unbounce tests.

## 6.2 Mobile-First Design

70%+ of search traffic is mobile. Google indexes mobile-first.

Checklist:
- **Touch targets ≥ 48×48 px** (Apple HIG: 44 pt; Google Material: 48 dp). Closer than 8 px apart and adjacent buttons get mistapped.
- **Font size ≥ 16 px** for body text (prevents iOS auto-zoom on inputs).
- **No horizontal scroll**.
- **Fast above-the-fold content** — defer everything else.
- **Thumb-friendly CTA placement** — bottom center or right is reachable; top corners aren't.
- **Avoid intrusive interstitials** — Google penalizes pop-ups that block content on mobile (excluding cookie consent and age gates).

## 6.3 Trust Signals

Subtle but compounding:
- **HTTPS everywhere** — ranking factor since 2014, table stakes now.
- **Affiliate disclosure** clearly visible — FTC requirement, also a trust marker.
- **Privacy policy, Terms of Service** linked in footer. Users (and Google) expect them.
- **Real editorial content** — not just API-fed product cards. Personality and voice signal a real operation.
- **Professional design** — typography, spacing, imagery quality. Cheap design = cheap impression = lower conversion.
- **Fast loading** — speed builds trust subconsciously. 100ms improvement = +8.4% conversion (Deloitte).
